 **Headline:** The brief at a *glance*.

**Clinic Type**  
 Specialist neurosurgery and spine practice

**Project Focus**  
 Brand and voice, website architecture, condition structure, healthcare SEO, and paid search

### **04 Clinic Genie diagnosis**

**Eyebrow:** CLINIC GENIE DIAGNOSIS  
 **Headline:** Four lenses. One *clearer picture*.

**Intro:**  
 We looked at the project through four lenses. How patients search, what they need to understand, where trust is built, and what makes them ready to enquire.

That produced a three-pillar structure taken straight from the clinic's own name, credentials translated into plain reassurance, and a tone calibrated for people making one of the hardest decisions of their lives.

 How patients look for care before they choose a specialist, and which queries signal real enquiry intent.

 Whether conditions are grouped, explained, and connected in a way patients can follow without guessing.

**Trust Signals**  
 Where credibility is built through structure, doctor context, hospital access, and responsible claims.

**Enquiry Readiness**  
 Whether call, WhatsApp, form, and booking actions appear at the moments patients are ready to act.

### **05 What we worked on**

**Eyebrow:** WHAT WE WORKED ON  
 **Headline:** The work behind the *clearer journey*.

**Intro:**  
 Five workstreams, one goal. Help patients move from search to understanding to enquiry with less friction.

**01 Brand and voice**  
 Built an identity around the three things the practice treats, then wrote a voice that stays calm without going cold. Serious subject matter, human delivery.

**02 Three-pillar architecture**  
 Turned the clinic's own name into its navigation. Brain, Spine, Nerves. Three doors, each leading to the conditions and treatments underneath.

**03 Copywriting**  
 Translated fellowships and sub-specialties into what a patient actually wants to know. Who is treating me, what have they done before, and what happens next.

**04 Search intent mapping**  
 Mapped high-volume everyday pain queries separately from low-volume, high-stakes surgical searches, so neither crowds the other out.

**05 Trust-led page flow**  
 Led with the pillar, then the condition, then the surgeon, then the four hospitals he operates from, before asking for the appointment.

### **09 Built for responsible healthcare marketing**

**Eyebrow:** BUILT FOR RESPONSIBLE HEALTHCARE MARKETING  
 **Headline:** Clarity without *overclaiming*.

**Intro:**  
 Neurosurgery is the category where irresponsible marketing does the most damage. A patient rushed into the wrong decision, or scared out of the right one, carries that for life. Every page was written with that weight in mind.

**Built within the rules**  
 Copy, structure, and claims were shaped around Singapore healthcare advertising guidelines from the first draft.

**Evidence-led language**  
 Claims stay grounded in what the practice can support. What cannot be substantiated is not said.

**No fear, no false promises**  
 No guaranteed outcomes, no success rates as marketing, no urgency applied to people who are already frightened.

**Reputation first**  
 Every page protects the surgeon's name while making complex care easier to understand and enquire about.
