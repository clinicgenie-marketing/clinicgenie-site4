### **05 What we worked on**

**Eyebrow:** WHAT WE WORKED ON  
 **Headline:** The work behind the *clearer journey*.

**Intro:**  
 Six workstreams, one goal. Help parents move from search to understanding to enquiry with less friction.

**01 Brand and voice**  
 Built a botanical identity around seeds, seasons, and growth, then carried it into the writing so consistently that even the closed sign has a reason. Rest is part of growth, too.

**02 Two-pillar architecture**  
 Separated general paediatrics from developmental and behavioural care, so everyday health and complex needs each get the structure and tone they deserve.

**03 Copywriting**  
 Wrote every page for a parent, not a clinician. Calm, plain, and specific about what happens at a visit, with no rush and no pressure.

**04 Search intent mapping**  
 Prioritised the searches parents actually type. Not just paediatrician Bukit Timah, but P1 deferment, SG Enable referral, SPED placement report, developmental assessment. High intent, high need, and very little competition writing about them clearly.

**05 FindClinic presence**  
 Listed and structured the clinic on FindClinic.sg, giving a brand new practice a discoverable, credible profile from opening week while its own domain built authority.

**06 Paid search**  
 Structured campaigns around vaccination, general consultation, and developmental assessment intent, tracked through to booking.

### **09 Built for responsible healthcare marketing**

**Eyebrow:** BUILT FOR RESPONSIBLE HEALTHCARE MARKETING  
 **Headline:** Clarity without *overclaiming*.

**Intro:**  
 Writing about children carries a duty that goes beyond compliance. A parent reading about developmental delay is already frightened. Every page was written to inform them without adding to that.

**Built within the rules**  
 Copy, structure, and claims were shaped around Singapore healthcare advertising guidelines and the Health Products Act from the first draft.

**Educational, never diagnostic**  
 Content helps parents recognise when to ask a question. It never suggests a diagnosis, a prognosis, or a medication decision online.

**No fear, no false promises**  
 No guaranteed outcomes, no developmental timelines presented as certainties, and no urgency applied to families already under strain.

**Reputation first**  
 Every page protects the paediatricians' names while making children's care easier to understand and enquire about.
