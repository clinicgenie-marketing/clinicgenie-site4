### **03 Before the work**

**Eyebrow:** BEFORE THE WORK  
 **Headline:** What stood in the *way*.

**Intro:**  
 Starting from nothing is a blank page, not a shortcut. Everything had to be conjured from scratch, and every decision had to earn its place in a competitive category.

**No brand, no presence**  
 A new clinic with no identity, no site, and no search footprint in a category where competitors had years of head start.

**Acne is not one condition**  
 Patients search comedonal, cystic, hormonal, rosacea, or simply "the red painful ones". A single treatment page could never answer all of them.

**Crowded category, thin differentiation**  
 Every aesthetic clinic in Singapore lists acne somewhere. Very few do only acne. That difference needed to be structural, not just a strapline.

### **05 What we worked on**

**Eyebrow:** WHAT WE WORKED ON  
 **Headline:** The work behind the *clearer journey*.

**Intro:**  
 Six workstreams, one goal. Help patients move from search to understanding to enquiry with less friction.

**01 Brand identity**  
 Created the name treatment, voice, and visual system around one idea. Face it. Fix it. Direct, unfussy, and built for a patient who is tired of being sold to.

**02 Condition architecture**  
 Structured the site around five acne types rather than one treatment list, so each patient lands on the page that matches their skin.

**03 Search intent mapping**  
 Mapped pages and campaigns around how patients actually describe acne, symptom first, diagnosis second.

**04 Trust-led page flow**  
 Built the four-step journey and doctor introduction so patients understand the care model before being asked to book.

**05 Conversion touchpoints**  
 Placed call, WhatsApp, and booking across the site at the points where patients are ready to act.

**06 Social video**  
 Produced educational Reels and TikTok content that carries the clinic voice and feeds the same condition topics the site ranks for.

### **09 Built for responsible healthcare marketing**

**Eyebrow:** BUILT FOR RESPONSIBLE HEALTHCARE MARKETING  
 **Headline:** Clarity without *overclaiming*.

**Intro:**  
 Acne marketing is where healthcare advertising rules get tested hardest. Before and after shots, outcome promises, and pressure tactics are everywhere in this category. We did not use any of them.

**Built within the rules**  
 Copy, structure, and claims were shaped around Singapore healthcare advertising guidelines from the first draft.

**No before and after**  
 The category leans on transformation imagery. We built trust through explanation, doctor context, and honest process instead.

**No false promises**  
 No guaranteed outcomes, no timelines to clear skin, no pressure-led patient messaging.

**Reputation first**  
 Every page protects the doctor's name while making acne care easier to understand and enquire about.
