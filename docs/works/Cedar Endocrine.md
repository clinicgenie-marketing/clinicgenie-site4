### **03 Before the work**

**Eyebrow:** BEFORE THE WORK  
**Headline:** What stood in the *way*.

**Intro:**  
The starting point was familiar. A respected specialist with genuine depth of expertise, and a digital presence that did not yet show it.

**Specialty before symptom**  
Endocrinology is a word patients rarely type. They search the condition, or the worry behind it. The structure needed to start where patients start.

**Wide scope, thin structure**  
Six distinct condition areas sat inside one broad specialty. Each needed its own page logic, keyword intent, and plain explanation.

**Two locations, one decision**  
Consulting across two hospitals is a strength. Presented unclearly, it becomes one more thing a patient has to work out before booking.

### **04 Clinic Genie diagnosis**

**Eyebrow:** CLINIC GENIE DIAGNOSIS  
 **Headline:** Four lenses. One *clearer picture*.

**Intro:**  
We looked at the project through four lenses. How patients search, what they need to understand, where trust is built, and what makes them ready to enquire.

That showed us where the brand needed a warmer voice, where the website needed stronger condition structure, and where enquiry points were missing at the moments that mattered.

**Search Intent**  
How patients look for care before they choose a clinic, and which queries signal real enquiry intent.

**Service Clarity**  
Whether conditions are grouped, explained, and connected in a way patients can follow without guessing.

**Trust Signals**  
Where credibility is built through structure, doctor context, location clarity, and responsible claims.

**Enquiry Readiness**  
Whether call, form, WhatsApp, and booking actions appear at the moments patients are ready to act.

### **05 What we worked on**

**Eyebrow:** WHAT WE WORKED ON  
 **Headline:** The work behind the *clearer journey*.

**Intro:**  
Five workstreams, one goal. Help patients move from search to understanding to enquiry with less friction.

**01 Brand identity**  
Built a calmer, warmer clinical identity around the idea of nurtured care, then carried it across web, print, and clinic collateral.

**02 Condition architecture**  
Structured six endocrine areas into clear pillars, with diabetes and thyroid expanded into supporting condition pages.

**03 Search intent mapping**  
Mapped pages and campaigns around what patients search first. The condition, not the specialty.

**04 Trust-led page flow**  
Reordered page sections so patients understand the doctor and the clinic before being asked to enquire.

**05 Conversion touchpoints**  
 Reviewed where call, WhatsApp, booking form, and appointment CTAs should appear across both locations.

### **09 Built for responsible healthcare marketing**

**Eyebrow:** BUILT FOR RESPONSIBLE HEALTHCARE MARKETING  
 **Headline:** Clarity without *overclaiming*.

**Intro:**  
 The work was designed to help patients understand the clinic while protecting the doctor's reputation and staying within healthcare advertising rules.

**Built within the rules**  
 Copy, structure, and claims were shaped around Singapore healthcare advertising guidelines from the first draft.

**Evidence-led language**  
 Claims stay grounded in what the clinic can support. What cannot be substantiated is not said.

**No false promises**  
 No guaranteed outcomes, exaggerated comparisons, or pressure-led patient messaging.

**Reputation first**  
 Every page protects the clinic's name while making care easier to understand and enquire about.
